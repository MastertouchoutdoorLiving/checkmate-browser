Connect your Messages

To show your iMessages and texts here, macOS needs to let the browser read your Messages history. This is a one-time step, and nothing leaves your Mac.

  1. Click Open Privacy Settings below.
  2. Under Full Disk Access, turn on Terminal. If it isn't listed, click + and add it from Applications (Terminal is in Applications → Utilities).
  3. Quit the browser (Cmd+Q) and open it again.
